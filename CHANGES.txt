THE CALCULATION
---------------
Computed on your phone from the sun's position. No API, no key, no network,
no new package - it works offline and on a plane.

I checked the maths against known sunrise and sunset before writing the
Dart, and it lands to the minute: Delhi 06:36 / 17:33, London 07:02 /
16:26, Makkah 06:26 / 17:42, Mumbai 06:41 / 18:03.

Then I checked it against YOUR sheet. Your times match a Mumbai longitude
with Shafi'i Asr, within a few minutes:

                  your sheet     calculated
   Fajr             5:22           5:26
   Zuhr            12:20          12:22
   Asr              3:41           3:38   (Shafi'i)
   Maghrib          6:08           6:03
   Isha             7:15           7:18

So your times are calculated ones, not mosque jamaat times. Expect the app
to sit within about five minutes of what you are already using.


A CORRECTION ON THE SCHOOLS
---------------------------
You asked for Shafi'i and Hanbali side by side. Those two always give the
SAME Asr - Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali all use one shadow-length. Only
Hanafi differs, using two, which puts it roughly 45 minutes later.

You chose to show all four named separately, so that is what it does: four
rows, three of them matching, with a line underneath explaining why. Tap
one to choose which drives the rest of the app.


WHAT THE SCREEN DOES
--------------------
  * Fajr, sunrise, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha for any date - arrows to step
    a day, tap the date to jump
  * "Asr by school": all four, your choice marked
  * a next-prayer card with the countdown
  * TAP ANY TIME TO CORRECT IT. The correction applies to THAT DAY ONLY,
    exactly as you asked - fix Fajr on 6 November and 7 November still
    calculates clean. Corrected times show in blue with an undo arrow.
  * the calculation method lives behind the tune icon: Karachi (the default
    and the usual choice in India), Muslim World League, ISNA, Egyptian,
    Umm al-Qura, Gulf, Tehran
  * "Follow my location" recalculates wherever you are, so travelling stays
    right. Turn it off to pin the times to one place.

Location is read only when you open the screen, never in the background,
and the coordinates never leave your phone - the sun maths is local.


WHY THE TRACKER WINDOWS ARE NOT WIRED TO THIS YET
-------------------------------------------------
Deliberate, and I would rather you agree before I do it: check the
calculated times against your mosque for a day or two first. If they are
off for your area, better to find that on a screen than to have your Fajr
habit silently re-timed and start marking you late.

Once you are happy, say so and drop 12 binds them: a habit gets a "this is
Fajr" setting, its window becomes the real window for that day (Fajr until
sunrise, Zuhr until Asr, and so on), it moves with you when you travel, and
your per-day corrections carry through to the tracker too.
