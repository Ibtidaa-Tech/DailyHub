1. MENTOR EDITS A LIVE RUN
--------------------------
Open a student, and there is now a + in the toolbar: adds a habit straight
into the run they are already doing. On the Sheet tab, tapping any column
header opens that habit for editing - rename it, change its window, adjust
the grace minutes.

The rules always allowed this (trackerItems is read+write for an accepted
mentor); only the buttons were missing.

The ticks themselves stay read-only. The habits are yours to set, the
record of doing them is theirs to make. Mentor ticking arrives in drop 08
as its own clearly-marked thing.

A habit the mentor adds mid-run counts from the day it was added, like any
other late addition - so it shows Day 1 while the rest of the sheet carries
on at Day 9.


2. ONE TRACKER FOR SEVERAL STUDENTS
-----------------------------------
Hub -> Mentor -> "Set one tracker for several". Tick the students (everyone
who has accepted is pre-ticked), then build the tracker once in the usual
editor.

Each student gets their OWN COPY with its own id. They are not sharing a
record: one student editing theirs, or ticking theirs, never touches
anyone else's. That is the right shape for a cohort running the same
21-day programme - you set it once, everyone owns their own run of it.

Students who have not accepted yet are left out of the list, because
without an accepted link there is no permission to write into their data.

Assigning a different tracker to a different student still works the same
way it did: open that student and use the other toolbar button.
