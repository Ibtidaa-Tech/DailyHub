1. THE CUT-OFF NAMES
--------------------
"Lend & b...", "Savings g...", "Focus tim...", "Priority m..." - the tile
label was capped at one line, and four columns on a phone is about 85px.

Labels now wrap to two lines in a fixed-height box, so every tile still
lines up whether its name wraps or not. Nothing is truncated any more.


2. ALPHABETICAL
---------------
Tiles are now sorted A-Z inside each section, by the TRANSLATED name, so
the order is right in Hindi too.

I kept the sections (Plan, Money, Life, Home, Work) and sorted within
them, rather than collapsing everything into one long A-Z list - the
sections are what tell you roughly where to look. If you would rather have
one flat alphabetical list with no sections at all, say so; it is a
one-line change.


3. WHY PRAYER TIMES WAS MISSING
-------------------------------
Not a bug. Your account has a saved module list in Firebase from before

  Hub -> Manage modules -> Prayer times      (and Where was I, below)

Both need switching on once, on each device-independent account.


4. WHERE WAS I
--------------
Hub -> Manage modules -> Where was I, then Hub -> Where was I.

One entry per day:
  * captured automatically the first time you open the app each day
  * a label you write yourself - "home", "college", "Nani's house"
  * an optional note - what you were doing there
  * Maps button, and a copy button for the raw coordinates

NO BACKGROUND TRACKING. The app records a fix when you open it and at no
other time, which is also why it is one row per day rather than a trail of
points through your afternoon. The automatic capture uses the quiet,
never-prompting location call, so opening the app will never throw a
permission dialog at you - until you grant location from the screen
itself, it simply does nothing.

There is no reverse geocoding, so a place has no name until you give it
one. That is deliberate as well as simpler: "Nani's house" is worth more
to you in six months than "Unnamed Road, Ward 12".

It lives in your own data and a mentor cannot reach it - the rules you
deployed grant them the three tracker collections and nothing else. A
location history is the most sensitive thing in this app and it is worth
being plain about where it goes.


5. SAVING A PLACE WHERE YOU ARE
-------------------------------
Places now has a crosshair button in the top bar: "Save where I am". It
opens the editor already fetching your position, so you type the name and
the note while it finds you.

The editor also has a location strip on every place, new or existing:
coordinates when there is a pin, "Use my location" to attach one, and an x
to remove it. Saved places with a pin keep working exactly as before, the
coordinates just make the Maps link land on the exact spot rather than on
a text search of the address.

The address box was already there and still is - that is your write-in
box for anything that is not a street address.
